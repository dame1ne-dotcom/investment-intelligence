How the scores work

Opportunity (0–100): starts at 50, adds or subtracts up to 25 for 7-day price change, up to 15 for 24-hour change, and up to 10 for trading-volume-to-market-cap ratio. Risk (0–100): starts at 25, adds up to 30 for negative 7-day change, up to 20 for negative 24-hour change, up to 15 for extreme 7-day moves, and up to 10 for low volume relative to market cap. Higher risk means more caution. This is a momentum/liquidity screen, not a valuation model.

Missing data produces “insufficient data”, not a fabricated score. Scores are snapshots and do not use backtesting, order-book data, fundamentals, or news.

How scores are calculated

Opportunity (0–100): starts at 50. Adds or subtracts up to 30 points for 30-day price momentum, and up to 20 points for distance below the recent high. The latter is only a potential valuation/rebound signal, not proof of a bargain.

Risk (0–100; higher = more risk): starts at 35. Adds up to 40 points for a decline from the recent high and up to 25 points for negative 30-day momentum; positive momentum can reduce risk by up to 15 points. These are price-only indicators and omit liquidity, fundamentals, fraud, macro conditions and other major risks.

Prices are manually entered and may become stale. Scores are not calibrated, backtested or comparable to probabilities. A high opportunity score never cancels a high risk score.

Market data information

Cryptocurrency quotes are fetched while the app is open. Stock and ETF prices use the site’s market-data connection. Quotes may be delayed or unavailable; no background monitoring or trades occur.

Supported automatic crypto quotes: BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, XRP, DOGE, BNB, AVAX, LINK, DOT, LTC.

Data and privacy

Data is stored in this browser using localStorage. It is not synced or encrypted, so avoid using a shared device. Clearing browser storage can delete it.

Never enter exchange credentials, brokerage passwords or wallet seed phrases.
